04 / ABOUT CAELIVIS

THE FUTURE OF
EXPLORATION IS
HUMAN.

CAELIVIS is a spaceflight health technology demonstration developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It explores how an offline-first interface could help people examine crew-entered health observations and trends in a long-duration mission context.

It is a concept demonstration—not a deployed NASA system, medical device, diagnostic tool, or source of astronaut telemetry.
